Open Kit
========

Offers the Padawan and Warrior kits, the locked wardrobe and the locked Armory
gear to every operator in your company, without telling anyone they are Tel-Rea
or Cly.

Requires
--------
- Star Wars: Zero Company, Steam build 24874058. Open Kit refuses to run on any
  other build rather than risk writing to the wrong addresses.
- UE4SS for Zero Company, installed first.

Install
-------
1. Close the game.
2. In Steam: right-click the game, Manage, Browse local files.
3. Go into SWZeroCompany, then Binaries, then Win64.
4. Drag the "ue4ss" folder from this archive into that Win64 folder and merge.

You should end up with:

  SWZeroCompany\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\ZCOMOpenKit\enabled.txt
  SWZeroCompany\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\ZCOMOpenKit\dlls\main.dll

Remove
------
Delete SWZeroCompany\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\ZCOMOpenKit.

Nothing is written to your save, so removing the mod removes the offer. This has
been tested: a save whose operators wear modded outfits loads cleanly with the
mod gone, the characters keep the look, and the parts are simply no longer in the
picker. That works because Open Kit adds no assets -- everything it offers ships
with the game already, and the mod only stops hiding it.

Specializations are the one case to be careful with. If you installed a "-swap"
pak, put everyone back on a stock specialization, talent and weapon and save
BEFORE removing the mod; otherwise the game may show that slot as locked. Without
a "-swap" pak, hero kits stay pinned once taken and there is nothing to do.

Options
-------
Open Kit reads an "options" folder next to enabled.txt. Each file is one switch,
and the file only has to exist -- its contents are ignored.

  options\padawan.ini            offer the Padawan kit
  options\warrior.ini            offer the Warrior kit
  options\padawan-secondary.ini  also offer Padawan as a SECOND specialization
                                 (needs the pairing container -- see below)
  options\padawan-saber.ini      also offer the two lightsabers as weapon
                                 classes -- OFF, and read the warning below
  options\wardrobe.ini           offer the Mandalorian armour sets (Man001,
                                 Man002) in the wardrobe
  options\wardrobe-cly.ini       also offer Cly Kullervo's own armour and both
                                 of her helmets
  options\wardrobe-jedi.ini      also offer Tel-Rea's Jedi robes, in two
                                 variants
  options\wardrobe-heroes.ini    also offer everyone else's clothes -- Hawks,
                                 Kabb, Jae, Baker, Luco, Trick, Dozen,
                                 Sawtooth, the Commander, Visser
  options\wardrobe-anakin.ini    also offer Anakin's Jedi outfit
  options\wardrobe-authored.ini  also offer the "authored only" wardrobe --
                                 the largest single group, 245 parts
  options\wardrobe-helmets.ini   also let NON-HUMAN operators wear the human
                                 helmets (read the note below)
  options\wardrobe-families.ini  offer every outfit family to every character --
                                 this is what lets Cly, Tel-Rea and the other
                                 story characters wear what Hawks wears
  options\wardrobe-story.ini     also offer clothes the campaign has not
                                 unlocked yet -- OFF by default, because it
                                 hands you rewards the save has not earned
  options\colours.ini            add 40 outfit colours and 90 weapon paints
                                 the game ships but does not offer you
  options\colours-extra.ini      add 95 more outfit colours on top of those
                                 (needs colours.ini)
  options\armoury-dc17m.ini      offer Trick's DC-17m Weapon System
  options\armoury-rex-pistol.ini offer Captain Rex's twin DC-17 pistols
  options\armoury-hero-weapons.ini  offer the other characters' weapons
  options\armoury-sabers.ini     offer the lightsaber hilts in the Armory
                                 (needs the saber-armoury pak, and
                                 padawan-saber.ini, to be visible at all)

If there is no "options" folder at all, the defaults apply: everything on except
wardrobe-story and padawan-saber. If the folder exists, it is taken literally --
a switch whose file is not there is off.

A switch this version does not recognise is logged and ignored rather than
treated as an error, so an older DLL survives a newer install.

The lightsabers, and why they are off
-------------------------------------
Turning on padawan-saber offers two lightsabers as weapon classes: Tel-Rea's,
which is held in two hands, and Anakin's, which is held in one.

There are two of them because which one looks right depends on the operator
carrying it, and the game gives no way to pick for you -- there is no gender
anywhere in its customization data, only species and body type. So both are
offered and you keep whichever animates correctly on your character.

Tested so far: Anakin's ONE-HANDED saber is the better of the two. It is held
correctly walking around the ship and it fights correctly in combat. Sprinting is
the one thing it gets wrong -- hold shift to run and the grip breaks until you
stop. Tel-Rea's two-handed one is worse on a male operator. Try both on the
operator you care about.

They work on an operator who already exists: you can equip one, it is carried on
the back, and it has been used through a full mission.

DO NOT pick one while CREATING a character. The weapon class step there will
show a saber but will not accept it -- no tick ever appears -- and if you leave
that step without a weapon class the character reaches the tutorial holding
nothing, unable to fire the shot the tutorial asks for. Choose an ordinary
blaster during creation and switch to a saber afterwards, from the operator
screen, where it works properly.

The kits themselves are unaffected either way. The Jedi and Mandalorian
specializations, their talents and their abilities all work with this switched
off.

The wardrobe
------------
653 parts in total, across five slots -- torso, legs, arms, boots and helmet.
wardrobe.ini alone is the sixteen Mandalorian parts from the two generic armour
families; the other switches widen it from there.

They are cosmetic: an outfit part in this game carries a mesh, a footstep sound
and, on the armoured legs, a wider stance. It carries no ability and no
attribute.

Tel-Rea's Jedi robes are offered too, under wardrobe-jedi. They were left out of
the first build on the theory that they would not draw on anyone but her -- each
piece ships a single mesh marked short, thin and feminine. That turned out to be
wrong: the body tags on a mesh are a best-match hint rather than a requirement,
so they draw on anybody. Whether they FIT your operator is your call, the same
way the two lightsaber grips are.

Colours
-------
The game ships 455 outfit colours and offers you 119 of them; the rest are
marked "authored only", the same lock the clothes were behind. colours.ini adds
40 of them plus 90 weapon paints, each with its own picker label so you can tell
Amber 02 from Amber 13.

colours.ini adds Sternab's 40, curated by eye. colours-extra.ini adds 95 more,
picked by measuring instead: every locked colour at least 0.05 away in linear
RGB from anything you can already choose. Together that is 135 of the 335.

The other 200 are left out because they are not really colours you do not have:
82 are within a rounding error of one already in your picker and 126 more are
too close to tell apart on a swatch. Six more are excluded by name -- four
developer test ramps and two shades belonging to a specific character.

All 90 locked weapon paints are offered by colours.ini; there is no extra set
for those, because there is nothing left.

This half of the mod is Sternab's Zero Company Expanded Colours, MIT licensed
and adapted with attribution. Do not run both -- they add the same colours
through the same hook.

Everyone's clothes, not just the Mandalorians'
----------------------------------------------
wardrobe-heroes offers every garment the game reserves to a named character:
Hawks's jackets, Kabb's gear, Jae's, Baker's, Luco's, Trick's, and the Dozen,
Sawtooth, Commander and Visser sets. wardrobe-anakin offers Anakin's outfit.

Some things are deliberately left out, and all of them for reasons read out of
the game's own data rather than chosen:

- Backpacks. A torso that should have one already carries it.
- Fathom's head. It is a face, not a garment.
- Five torsos -- Kabb's three and Tel-Rea's full robe -- that replace the
  wearer's BODY rather than dressing it. Kabb is a Herglic; his torso would put
  his body on your operator.

Why the story characters had less than Hawks
--------------------------------------------
The right to wear a family of clothes comes from your character's CLASS, and the
authored heroes have much poorer classes than the people you recruit:

  Hawks and every recruit    14 outfit families
  Luco                       11
  Cly                         1  -- Mandalorian only
  Tel-Rea                     0  -- none at all

That is why Cly could not wear most of what Hawks could. wardrobe-families
offers every family to everybody, which levels them up to Hawks rather than
levelling anyone down. It costs Hawks nothing.

Why two operators saw different wardrobes
-----------------------------------------
Because 75 helmets require your operator's species to be one the helmet fits,
and only Human, Clone, Mirialan and Umbaran qualify. A Rodian, Togruta, Zabrak,
Twi'lek, Weequay, Devaronian, Ovissian or Neimoidian was offered a much smaller
list, and Felshi, Tognath and droids were offered none at all.

wardrobe-helmets lifts that, so every operator sees the same helmets. This is
the one switch here that changes a FIT rather than a permission: a helmet shaped
for a human skull now has to sit on montrals, lekku, horns or a snout. Some
combinations will look wrong. That is a look rather than a bug, and it is your
call -- delete the file if you would rather have the smaller, tidier list.

What is still not offered, and why
----------------------------------
- Anything requiring the Deluxe or preorder edition. Those 66 parts are content
  sold separately; this mod does not hand them out.
- Eight parts needing a non-human skeleton -- Kabb's gear wants a Herglic body.
  The game refuses these for your operators and it is right to.
- Sixteen clone-boot tint variants whose data this mod could not read reliably
  enough to know which slot they belong in. Better absent than in the wrong list.
- Thirteen parts of the Clo009 and Plague Trooper sets, which claim to be arm
  wear while being named and modelled as torsos, helmets, boots and legs.
  Offering those would have put a torso on your operator's forearms.
- Backpacks, which a torso already carries, and five costumes that replace the
  wearer's whole body rather than dressing it.

Mandalorian helmets get a render fit so a wide face does not clip through the
visor. Cly's and Tel-Rea's helmets do not have one yet, because nobody has
measured what they need; report it if one of them clips.

It does not fix the lightsaber grip. That was worth checking, because it was
reported elsewhere that wearing Tel-Rea's outfit gives an operator her saber
animations; it does not, and the game's own data says why. An animation set is
chosen by the WEAPON a character carries, never by what they are wearing, and
Tel-Rea's outfit parts carry no animation and contribute no tags to anyone. See
docs/INVESTIGATION.md if you want the working.

Her outfit is not offered here in any case. It is authored for exactly one
body: each piece ships a single mesh, marked short, thin and feminine, so on
anyone else there would be nothing to draw. The Mandalorian armour ships both a
masculine and a feminine mesh, which is why it can be offered to your whole
company.

Why there is no Anakin or Rex class
-----------------------------------
Both exist in the game's data, and neither is offered here, because neither is a
player specialization. The game writes its tactical specializations in two
families: the ones you can be given require a "slot" tag, and the ones belonging
to scripted characters require a capability tag instead. Anakin and Rex are in
the second family -- alongside every enemy in the game.

The Jedi and Mandalorian kits are in the first. They are ordinary player
specializations that happen to be reserved for one character by name, which is
why this mod can offer them without pretending anyone is somebody else. Anakin's
is not, and offering it would mean claiming your operator is a scripted pawn.

About the second specialization slot
------------------------------------
The Jedi and Mandalorian kits are FIRST specializations. That is the game's
decision, not a limitation of this mod: every stock specialization ships a
separate "_Secondary" part that the game equips when you pick it for the second
slot, and Padawan and Warrior are the only two with no such part anywhere in the
game. Offered as a second specialization they come out with no abilities.

Padawan can be made to work there with the optional pairing container, which
tells the picker to use PadawanExtended -- the nearest thing the game ships --
as its second-slot form. Warrior has no equivalent and is first-slot only.

You can add or delete these files whenever you like without reinstalling.

The Armory
----------
Four switches offer gear the Armory does not normally show you.

armoury-dc17m offers Trick's DC-17m Weapon System -- a complete weapon with
three fire modes, its own abilities, attributes, animations and icon.

armoury-rex-pistol offers Captain Rex's twin DC-17s. These are the only
dual-wield kit in the game, and the only part granting the DualFire ability. Pick
the tile labelled "DC-17 (Rex)"; the stock single-pistol kit is labelled "DC-17"
and is easy to confuse with it, which is exactly why the labels were added.

armoury-hero-weapons offers the other named characters' weapons.

armoury-sabers offers the lightsaber hilts, and needs the most from you: the
saber-armoury pak must be installed, and padawan-saber.ini must be on, or there
is nothing to see. Even then the Inquisitor hilt can be looked at and not
selected on an ordinary operator -- the game gates it on being an authored
character, and no tag this mod can add satisfies that. It was measured rather
than assumed; see docs/INVESTIGATION.md.

Some Armory tiles draw the wrong picture -- Rex's DC-17 and every melee hilt show
another weapon's art, and the model preview says IMAGE MISSING. Those parts ship
no icon of their own and the game substitutes rather than drawing blank. The
weapon that actually equips is correct. Cosmetic, and not fixed.

How it works, and what it does not do
-------------------------------------
Open Kit adds parts to the lists the game offers you. It changes nothing about
any character.

That matters because of how this game finds parts. A character's name is not a
label on them -- it is a lookup key, and hero content is found VIA the name tags
a character carries. Give an operator a hero's name and you give them that hero's
whole character: their animations, their weapons, and in some cases their body.

So Open Kit never writes a name onto anybody. When you pick a locked part, it
asks the game's own requirement check on a throwaway copy of your character's
tags, lifts exactly the one gate that part needs, and destroys the copy. Every
other requirement is still answered about the real you -- an Astromech is still
refused Mandalorian armour, as authored, and the mod never learns it happened.

The cost of doing it this way is that it has to be a code mod. A pak alone
cannot add to any of these lists, because they are all assembled in the game's
own code. That is the trade: Open Kit needs UE4SS and is pinned to a game build,
where a data-only mod would not be.

The wardrobe here derives from Sternab's Zero Company Mandalorian Wardrobe, which
is MIT licensed and came first: https://github.com/Sternab/ZeroCompanyMandoWardrobe
Run one or the other, not both -- they offer the same parts through the same
seam. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

Trouble
-------
Check SWZeroCompany\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\UE4SS.log for a line starting
[ZCOM_OPEN_KIT]. "READY" means it is running. "REFUSED" names the reason, and
the usual one is a game update this build has not been verified against.

Source, and the investigation behind it, are at the project's repository. MIT
licensed, in both directions.
